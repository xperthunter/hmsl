1. folders in the computer
One of my favorite movie line is from Zoolander "The files are in the computer". The protagonist is trying to prove some nefarious plot. The protagonist though is wonderfully clueless, so when the character is told that information he seeks is in the computer, he takes this directive quite literally. 
In a hilarious display of idiocy, he throws the villains computer to the ground, cracking it open and shattering the computing machine to pieces. The protagonist thinks there are literal folders of files inside the computer... he is right certainly there are files in the computer, but just not how he thinks they exist!
This class is certainly about the files in your computer. Instead of smashing our computers, no matter how much we want to sometimes, we need to learn and understand indeed how these files are arranged in our computer.
